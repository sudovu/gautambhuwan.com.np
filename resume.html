CURRICULUM VITAE

Bhuwan Gautam

NETWORK & SYSTEMS ENGINEER // (VHUWON MATHERS • @sudovu) // GAUTAMBHUWAN.COM.NP

Professional Profile

Bhuwan Gautam (also known as Vhuwon Mathers / @sudovu) is an accomplished Network & Systems Engineer with 5+ years of progressive hands-on experience in Internet Service Provider (ISP) network infrastructure (advancing from NOC Trainee to NOC Administrator) alongside deep expertise in internal enterprise networks, multi-site SD-WAN, and zero-trust campus separation & isolation for schools, colleges, corporate business houses, metropolitan city offices, and local municipalities. Specializes in carrier GPON/EPON OLT deployments, enterprise switching fabrics (Cisco Catalyst, Juniper EX), multi-branch SD-WAN (Fortinet Secure SD-WAN, IPsec overlay), VRF-Lite micro-segmentation, Private VLANs, core BGP peering & internal OSPF multi-area routing, Carrier-Grade NAT (CGNAT RFC 6598), and next-generation perimeter firewalls (FortiGate, pfSense HA).

Technical Competencies

Enterprise LAN, SD-WAN & Network Isolation:

Fortinet Secure SD-WAN, Cisco Meraki / Viptela, MikroTik IPsec overlay, dynamic SLA path steering, VRF-Lite, 802.1Q micro-segmentation, Private VLANs (PVLANs), 802.1X NAC, RADIUS, isolated Guest Wi-Fi captive portals, and CCTV/IoT physical containment.

Campus & Public Sector Deployments:

Specialized architectures for Educational Institutions (Schools & Colleges), Corporate Business Houses, Metropolitan City Administrations, and Local Municipalities (e-Governance data centers and ward interconnects).

ISP & Optical Access (FTTH):

Huawei SmartAX (MA5608T), ZTE (C320), VSOL GPON/EPON OLTs, ONT/ONU provisioning, DBA profiles (Type 1–5), T-CONTs, GEM ports, QinQ (802.1ad) VLAN tagging, OTDR fiber testing, PPPoE / BRAS.

Core Routing & Switching:

Cisco IOS-XE (Catalyst 3850/9300, ASR), Juniper Junos (MX204, SRX345, EX3400/4300), MikroTik RouterOS (CCR), BGP (eBGP/iBGP, Communities, NPIX Peering), OSPFv2/v3, MSTP (802.1s), LACP (802.3ad).

Perimeter Security & CGNAT:

Fortinet FortiGate, pfSense Plus HA (CARP), Carrier-Grade NAT (CGNAT RFC 6598 100.64.0.0/10), IPsec IKEv2 Site-to-Site VPNs, Zone-Based Firewalls, DHCP Snooping, Dynamic ARP Inspection (DAI), Storm Control.

NMS Telemetry & Automation:

Zabbix, LibreNMS, Grafana, PRTG, SNMPv2c/v3, NetFlow/sFlow, Wireshark, Linux (Ubuntu/Debian), Nginx reverse proxy, Docker, Anycast DNS, Python (Netmiko), Bash scripting.
