SERVICES & SCOPE

Areas of Technical Practice

Practical domains of expertise where I design, configure, and troubleshoot network and system infrastructure.

PRACTICE // 01

Enterprise Network Architecture & Design

Designing structured Layer 2 and Layer 3 network topologies tailored for branch offices and multi-subnet corporate environments.

  • VLAN segmentation and 802.1Q trunking layouts
  • OSPF multi-area routing and route summarization
  • Redundant default gateway configurations
  • IPv4/IPv6 address hierarchy and allocation schemes
PRACTICE // 02

Linux Server Deployment & Hardening

Deploying and configuring reliable Linux servers with security-first operational standards.

  • Ubuntu / Debian production installation and hardening
  • Nginx reverse proxy and SSL/TLS automation
  • SSH bastion configurations and key-based enforcement
  • UFW and iptables packet filtering configurations
PRACTICE // 03

DNS Architecture & Web Delivery

Connecting domains with resilient authoritative DNS setups and high-speed edge delivery platforms.

  • Authoritative Anycast DNS zone management
  • A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, and TXT record optimization
  • Automated SSL certificate provisioning and HTTPS enforcement
  • Serverless edge static site deployment via GitHub Pages
PRACTICE // 04

Infrastructure Telemetry & Diagnostic Support

Proactive health tracking, device unbinding/management, and structured troubleshooting methodology.

  • CCTV and hardware device management (Hik-Partner Pro)
  • Automated endpoint and ping heartbeat probes
  • Diagnostic packet trace inspection and route isolation
  • Thorough network documentation and topology diagrams
PRACTICE // 05

Enterprise, Campus & Municipal Infrastructure

Turnkey internal network design and deployment engineered specifically for educational campuses (schools, colleges), corporate business houses, and municipal/metropolitan local government offices.

  • High-density school and college Wi-Fi & computer lab networks
  • Corporate business house multi-floor structured LAN distribution
  • Municipality and metropolitan e-Governance data center & ward connectivity
  • Redundant campus core switching rings and fiber distribution backbones
PRACTICE // 06

SD-WAN, Network Separation & Zero-Trust Isolation

Connecting distributed branches with intelligent SD-WAN overlays while enforcing strict internal micro-segmentation to isolate critical data from guest and untrusted endpoints.

  • Multi-branch SD-WAN (Fortinet Secure SD-WAN, Cisco, MikroTik) with auto-failover
  • VRF-Lite and 802.1Q VLAN separation across administrative, financial, and student zones
  • Private VLANs (PVLANs) preventing lateral movement across workstations and labs
  • 802.1X NAC, isolated Guest Wi-Fi captive portals, and CCTV/IoT physical containment
