✉️ Free Domain Mail Server & DNS Guide

Your domain gautambhuwan.com.np is already receiving emails through Zoho Mail's free tier. Below are the exact, copyable DNS records to configure in ClouDNS for 100% inbox delivery (preventing spam filters) and connecting the website's contact form.

Open Zoho Mail Inbox →
1. MX RECORDS (INBOUND EMAIL) ACTIVE ON CLOUDNS

Directs incoming mail sent to @gautambhuwan.com.np to Zoho servers:

Priority 10: mx.zoho.com
Priority 20: mx2.zoho.com
Priority 50: mx3.zoho.com
2. SPF RECORD (SENDER POLICY) ADD TO CLOUDNS

Tells Gmail and Outlook that Zoho is authorized to send emails for your domain:

Type: TXT | Host: @
Value: v=spf1 include:zoho.com ~all
3. DMARC RECORD (SECURITY POLICY) ADD TO CLOUDNS

Protects your domain from email spoofing and phishing impersonation:

Type: TXT | Host: _dmarc
Value: v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:info@gautambhuwan.com.np
4. DKIM RECORD (DIGITAL SIGNATURE) ZOHO CONSOLE

Cryptographically signs your emails. In Zoho Mail Control Panel → Email Configuration → DKIM, generate your domain key selector zoho._domainkey and add the TXT record to ClouDNS.
